Recently I had the opportunity to speak at [NEJS CONF](https://nejsconf.com/) about javascript decorators.  It was an awesome experience.  JavaScript decorators give you an easy way to markup your code with a declarative.  They are now used by most of the new frontend frameworks, but you can also write your own.  You can learn how by watching my [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhJZv-nQOzM) or by looking at my [slides](https://nmackey.com/presentation-decorators/) and [examples](https://github.com/nmackey/presentation-decorators-examples).
